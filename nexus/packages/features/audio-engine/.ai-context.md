# Audio Engine Context

**Core**: WebAudio Graph + Policy Decision + CORS Detection
**Graph**: `Source→Gain→Bass→EQ[10]→Comp→P@n→Delay→Analyse→Dest`

## Decision Mode v3.0

- **Advanced**: vol>100 | EQ!=0 | comp | bass | pan | delay | viz
- **NativeLite**: !Advanced
- **NativeWebAudio**: Advanced & !Restricted
- **Capture**: Advanced & Restricted & Enabled (Predictive Only)

## Implementation Notes

- **CORS**: Analyser observation, 6s threshold. `SAFE` results bypass repeated checks.
- **Predictor**: `predictCapture` depends on `enabled`, `isRestricted`, and `needsAdvancedProcessing`.
