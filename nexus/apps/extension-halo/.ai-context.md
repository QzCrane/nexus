Tree: src/{entry, components, kernel, tools, i18n}

- Dev: regex, userscripts, yaml, diff, code-formatter, csv-viewer, ip, jwt, sql, protobuf, dom-selector...
- Efficiency: smooth-scroll(v2), tabs, pdf, clipboard(monitor), pomodoro, session-manager...
- Security/Media/Browser: password-gen, totp, image-tools, cookie, reader, translate...
