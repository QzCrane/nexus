# HALO 上下文 (v1.7.0)

形态: SidePanel | 核心: 零追踪/本地优先 | 定位: 取代 He3 的浏览器原生开发套件

## 🏗️ 架构概要 (详细见 docs/architecture.md)

Auto-Registry: manifest.json → generate-registry → \_generated.ts & loader.ts
Tree: src/{entry, components(sidepanel/Context), kernel(utils/unified-search), tools, i18n(i18n-core)}

## 🛠️ 工具箱 (✅ 83 tools)

Core: qrcode, color-picker, json-formatter, hash, uuid, timestamp, url, store, settings...
Dev: regex, **userscripts(Pro:编辑/排序/JS+CSS/导入导出/Header 解析)**, yaml, diff, code-formatter, csv-viewer, ip, jwt, sql, protobuf, dom-selector...
Efficiency: smooth-scroll(v2), tabs, pdf, clipboard(monitor), pomodoro, session-manager...
Security/Media/Browser: password-gen, totp, image-tools, cookie, reader, translate...

## 🔌 核心服务 (v1.7.0)

- Search: unified-search.ts (全语言+Pinyin+多字段+registerSearchLocale 扩展)
- State: SidePanelContext (消除 prop drilling) | useToolState (sync/local/debounce)
- Preload: useIdlePreload (空闲批量预加载) | requestIdleCallback
- i18n: waitForI18n() Promise (Suspense-ready)

## 🔒 Red Lines

- Msg: HALO*TOOL_ACTIVATE | HALO_RULER*\* | HALO_CLIPBOARD_ADD
- 禁: 工具内独立 Storage 监听 | 动态 Padding (Layout Shift) | 资源未 detach
- 强: 消费 EngineStatusProvider & ActiveServicesProvider | 函数 ≤150 行 | 英文注释
- 类型: ToolMeta 统一 (消除 ModuleMeta 冗余) | SystemToolId (settings/module-store)
